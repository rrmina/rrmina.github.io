Rusty Mina

I’m a Machine Learning Researcher at GCash, a leading fintech company in the Philippines. Prior to joining GCash, I was a graduate student and ML researcher at KAIST, where I worked on continual learning and fairness in machine learning.

My research focuses on theoretical machine learning algorithms. Recently, I’ve been interested in developing tabular deep learning models and federated learning algorithms for real-world applications.

Keywords: Continual Learning, Tabular Deep Learning, Federated Learning
